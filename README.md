# MARYLENE REALTOR

Build a luxury personal-brand website for MARYLENE REALTOR — Marylene Maglio, a certified and registered realtor, property manager, and Mayan Riviera lifestyle ambassador based in Mexico's Riviera Maya. She is French, multilingual (French, English, Spanish), and known for impeccably produced Instagram real estate videos (@marylene_realtor). The site must feel glossy, posh, editorial, and timeless — think French luxury maison (Hermès, Vogue Paris editorial) meets high-end coastal real estate, with only the subtlest tropical warmth. Never kitschy, never "beach agency."

BRAND SYSTEM (apply globally, never deviate):

Palette: warm ivory/off-white (
#FAF7F2) backgrounds; deep espresso-ink (
#1C1A17) for text and footer; champagne gold (
#B99A5F) as the single accent for lines, buttons, hover states; one restrained coastal note — a muted Caribbean lagoon tone (
#9FC5BF) used sparingly (thin rules, small labels only).
Typography: elegant high-contrast serif for headlines (Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display), letter-spaced uppercase sans-serif for labels/nav (Inter or Jost, 0.15em tracking), clean sans for body. Generous whitespace everywhere.
Wordmark: "MARYLENE" in serif caps, "REALTOR" beneath in small tracked-out sans caps with thin gold rules either side. Monogram "MM" in a thin-line circle for favicon and footer.
Motion: slow, subtle — gentle fade-and-rise on scroll, slight image zoom on hover. Nothing bouncy.
Photography treatment: full-bleed imagery, slight warm grade. Use elegant placeholder images of Riviera Maya architecture, cenotes, white-stone villas, turquoise coastline.

PAGES & STRUCTURE:

Home
Full-screen hero: full-bleed background video/image placeholder of a Riviera Maya luxury property, dark gradient overlay. Overline in tracked caps: "RIVIERA MAYA · MEXICO". Headline: "Where the Caribbean Meets Home." Sub-line: "Luxury real estate, property management, and life on the Mayan Riviera — guided in French, English & Spanish." Two buttons: "Explore Properties" (gold, solid) and "Watch the Films" (ghost/outline).
Slim credibility bar: "Certified & Registered Realtor · Property Sales & Management · Playa del Carmen — Tulum — Cancún · FR / EN / ES".
Intro editorial section: large serif pull-quote style intro about Marylene with an elegant portrait placeholder, offset asymmetric layout, thin gold rule.
"The Films" section: 3-video grid of embedded videos (16:9 and 9:16 mixed, placeholder embeds), with a "View All Films" link.
Featured properties: 3-card row, minimal cards — image, location label in lagoon tone, price, one line, no clutter.
Services strip: three columns — Buy & Sell, Property Management, Relocation & Investment Guidance — each with a thin-line icon and two-line description.
"La Riviera" teaser: split section, image left, short lifestyle copy right, linking to the Riviera Maya guide page.
Testimonial carousel: single large serif quote at a time, client name and origin (e.g., "— C. Dubois, Montréal").
Final CTA: full-width ink-dark section, serif headline "Begin Your Riviera Story.", gold button "Book a Private Consultation".
About — "Meet Marylene"
Editorial magazine layout: large portrait, story of a French professional who made the Mayan Riviera home; her philosophy of refined, personal, trilingual service; credentials list (Certified & Registered Realtor, Property Sales & Management, collaboration with LATITUD 365 Properties). Elegant timeline or three-pillar layout: The Realtor · The Property Manager · The Ambassador.
Properties
Filterable minimal grid (location: Playa del Carmen, Tulum, Cancún, Puerto Aventuras; type: villa, condo, land, pre-construction; buy/invest). Large imagery, hover zoom, clean detail pages with gallery, specs, and "Enquire" form. Include 6 elegant placeholder listings with realistic Riviera Maya pricing in USD.
Property Management
Dedicated service page: owner-focused copy (rental management, maintenance, guest experience, reporting), a "for owners abroad" angle for Canadian/US/European owners, simple 3-step "How it works", CTA to request a management proposal.
Films / Media
A cinematic video gallery page titled "The Films". Grid of embedded videos with category filters (Property Tours, Riviera Life, Client Stories). Prominent Instagram follow card for @marylene_realtor. Use placeholder video embeds I will replace.
Riviera Maya Guide — "La Riviera"
Ambassador/lifestyle page: elegant area guides (Playa del Carmen, Tulum, Puerto Aventuras, Akumal, Cancún) as editorial cards; a short "Why the Riviera Maya" investment section (tourism growth, lifestyle, accessibility from North America & Europe) written elegantly, not salesy.
Contact
Split layout: form (name, email, phone, I'm interested in [buying / selling / property management / investing], preferred language FR/EN/ES, message) + direct details, WhatsApp button, Instagram link, office area "Riviera Maya, Q.R., Mexico". Calendly-style "Book a Consultation" button placeholder.

GLOBAL ELEMENTS:

Sticky transparent nav over hero, turning ivory on scroll. Nav: Home, Meet Marylene, Properties, Property Management, The Films, La Riviera, Contact. Right side: small FR | EN | ES language switcher (visual for now) and a gold "Consultation" button.
Footer: ink-dark, MM monogram, tagline "Luxury Real Estate · Mayan Riviera", nav links, Instagram/WhatsApp/email icons, note "In collaboration with LATITUD 365 Properties", legal line.
Fully responsive, mobile-first polish; 9:16 videos must look intentional on mobile.
SEO-ready: semantic headings, meta title "Marylene Maglio | Luxury Realtor & Property Management — Riviera Maya, Mexico".

Language: English for now (FR/ES coming later). Tone of all copy: warm, assured, editorial, concise — French refinement, zero clichés, no exclamation marks.
